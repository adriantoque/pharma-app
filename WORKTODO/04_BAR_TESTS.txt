OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Update the two test files that assert on the bottom bar, so they guard the new
four-tab layout instead of the old five-tab one.

This is not busywork. nav_alignment_test.dart exists for one reason: catch a
destination list and a branch list that have drifted out of order. Its own
header says so, and it is right -- `goBranch` takes an index, not a route name,
so a mismatch does not throw, it just shows the wrong screen. Four tabs is a
bigger reshuffle than the app has had so far, which makes this the moment that
guard is most worth having right.

Seven tests in widget_test.dart break. Six assertions in nav_alignment_test.dart
change. The other ~20 tests in widget_test.dart are untouched and must stay that
way -- if you find yourself editing one outside this list, check whether it
really depends on the bar.
